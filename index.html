How to write notes
  • Write the notes as they appear in music for your instrument, separated by spaces: G A Bb C#
  • Add an octave number for an exact note: C5. Add | for a bar line.
  • Start a line with # to name a part, like # Chorus.
  • Pasting from a website? Lines of lyrics are skipped, ^C means one octave up, and dashed notes like D-C-Bb stay together.
